# Open Pit Fleet — 1.0, 2026-10-19

Build all linked tools: `python verification/build-fleet.py`. This calls the support bilingual builder, then adds fleet navigation to all generated pages. Run from repository root: `node verification/fleet-test.mjs`, `python verification/fleet-independent.py`.

This is a preliminary deterministic capacity and cost model for specified equipment classes, not an OEM model selector or an approved fleet/design study. All default technical and commercial inputs are illustrative. Russian UI, local calculations, optional project input JSON export/import, standalone HTML report, CSV and print/PDF. Inquiry links prepare email/WhatsApp messages for the user's review and manual sending; source tag OPEN-PIT-FLEET-RU. No visit analytics or backend collection.

Method: bank density and swell are converted to loose density; truck payload is the minimum of weight and body-volume constraints. Integer bucket cycles, last bucket partial with full cycle time. Each independent group has one excavator and k trucks; steady group rate is load*60/max(loading time, base truck cycle/k). Excess-truck waiting is max(0,k*loading time-base truck cycle). Common system availability/usage provide the synchronized group clock; do not use this as an independent per-machine downtime simulation. Auxiliary equipment has a separate effective clock. Utilization excludes delays already included in cycles.

Dozer quantities are loose volume for a user-defined fraction of all mass, using a measured/assumed blade prism and push/return cycle. No compaction, ripping or road maintenance. Vertical drilling only: volume per hole=B*S*H, length=H+subdrill, cycle adds penetration and repositioning; whole holes per rig/year rounded down, required holes rounded up. Diameter is recorded for review, not used to design the blast pattern. Actual production is the minimum of target and all service capacities. Ore/waste output is proportional to the plan; both streams share one density/route/application.

V1 enumerates 1–40 excavators, 1–60 trucks each, max 800 total trucks; auxiliary machines max 100 per class. It selects minimum EAC meeting the target with entered capacity margin for these fixed equipment parameters. This is not a global optimum across brands, sizes, routing or dispatch policies. No reserve machines assigned. If search limits or physical bucket/payload incompatibility occur, no automatic result is supplied. A V0 shortfall remains visible and costs per tonne use its actual output, not target.

Financial scope: new purchase at time zero, fixed annual production and real unit rates, no replacement through the selected period; salvage at the end. Annual cash OPEX includes active-cycle hours (with modeled waiting), staffed-calendar wages, variable supplies and fixed ownership-related expenses. EAC additionally annualizes purchase net of discounted salvage. CAPEX is shown separately, not counted again in annual cost. PV is equipment costs only, not project NPV/profit. Excludes blasting consumables, roads/civil works, crushing/processing, dewatering, taxes and general mine overhead.

Sources in the UI: Caterpillar Hauling Solutions (pass match and geometric compatibility); Caterpillar Performance Handbook Ed.50, June 2022, section 13 pp13-23–25 and section16 production estimation (principles, not digitized curves); A system-wide approach to minimize the operational cost of bench production (2018), section2.1.1 Eq5 and Eq7; NIST HB135e2025 section3.2.1/3.2.2.1 for discount factors (not US federal rates). Route grades are descriptive, not a rimpull/retarding calculation. OEM performance and braking verification, TKPH, road geometry, face scheduling and jurisdictional safety review remain necessary.

Validation: 37 Node checks (reference case, constrained integer search, no feasible cheaper case in the stated domain, mass/capacity/hour and cost balances, zero tasks, unavailable drilling, zero output, body-volume payload, oversized bucket, queue saturation, distance sensitivity, zero interest, invalid inputs). Independent Python calculation reproduces cycle, capacities, integer hole quantities, hours, cash OPEX, CAPEX, PV and EAC. These validate this mathematical model, not field productivity or OEM suitability.
